I am Kai Visser, an independent design director and creative technologist. I work with ventures at the collision point of cutting-edge technology and cultural irreverence.

I believe modern web design has become clinically bored with itself—trapped in 12-column template libraries and beige components. My mission is building interfaces that demand physical presence.

CREDO // 01

Typography As Architecture

Type is not merely text to be read; it is the physical skeleton and focal tension of the screen. We mix dramatic scales, brutal monospace details, and couture italics to give software an indelible voice.

CREDO // 02

Intentional Kinetic Friction

Frictionless design makes apps forgettable. Purposeful physical motion, weight inertia, magnetic cursor resistance, and acoustic response transform cold software into a memorable tactile object.

CREDO // 03

Radical Asymmetry

Symmetrical cards exist to optimize templates, not to captivate human eyes. We break grids deliberately to direct the eye's path through surprising hierarchy and cinematic negative space.

PROJECT DISSECTION // 01

AURA Spatial OS

Veloce Labs Zurich
Timeline

8 Months (2025)

Role

Lead Design Architect

Outcome

+340% User Precision

The Architectural Challenge

Traditional spatial headsets mimic physical floating flat screens in virtual space. Veloce Labs tasked us with completely breaking free of desktop skeuomorphism and engineering an intuitive volumetric operating system.

"We discarded windows and buttons entirely. In our interface, spatial depth is your primary navigation axis."

Radical Design Solution

We established an optical typography framework where focal points are illuminated by micro-radiance nodes. Gaze tracking accelerates typographic tracking, giving the user immediate tactile awareness before finger gestures are ever initiated.
